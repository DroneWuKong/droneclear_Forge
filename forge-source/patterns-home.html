Reading discipline

How to read Patterns

The system is strongest when assessment, evidence, and coverage are read together. A score alone is a prioritization aid, not a conclusion.

1Read the assessment

What changed, over what window, and which dimension moved?

2Open the evidence

Review independent sources, direct records, contradictions, and provenance.

3Check coverage and confidence

Confirm freshness, collection gaps, source concentration, and attribution limits.

Important: Article mentions are not confirmed incidents. Relationship proximity is not an allegation. Weak indexed procurement evidence does not prove that no program, funding, or capability exists.
