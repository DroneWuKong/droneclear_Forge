Developer evidence details

Decoded messages use a reviewed subset of MAVLink common definitions. Unknown frames remain in raw capture and timestamped telemetry export. CRC checks detect corruption; signatures are preserved but not authenticated. Filtered/routed sequence gaps do not establish radio packet loss. Arrival time is local; device clocks and media pipeline delays are separate.
