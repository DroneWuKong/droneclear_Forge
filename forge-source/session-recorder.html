Help & offline app

Sharing a terminal or command-line window

Open and restore the terminal, then choose Add app window and the window in the browser picker. Chromium on Windows skips invisible or minimized windows. If the terminal is still missing, choose Add monitor and show it on that monitor.

The browser and OS supply the picker. The app's window/monitor preference cannot add windows the system excludes. The standalone app currently uses this same browser picker.

Capture limits and timing

Up to eight video inputs. More inputs use more CPU and storage. Capture stops at 192 MiB, 30,000 timeline entries, 12 MiB of decoded timeline or two hours. ZIPs support 256 MiB. Download before clearing browser data. Source clocks, pipeline delays and alignment uncertainty remain separate; recordings do not certify airworthiness or standards compliance.
