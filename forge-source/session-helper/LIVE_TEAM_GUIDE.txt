LIVE FIELD DEVELOPMENT SESSION

1. Start the standalone recorder before the call. Select the GCS/app window,
   camera or USB video input, and a copy of local MAVLink telemetry.
2. Start recording locally. Join your normal Teams or Google Meet meeting.
3. Select Live team view in the recorder. Share this recorder window or tab
   using the meeting's sharing controls. The team sees previews, reported
   vehicle observations, their ages, the local recording clock and marked events.
4. Mark reproduction steps, unexpected behavior and team feedback as it happens.
   Choose Session workspace to return to setup/replay without ending capture.
5. If the meeting or internet connection drops, keep the recorder running.
   Local camera/screen, USB/localhost telemetry, notes and saves do not use the
   meeting connection. Remote viewers reconnect through the existing meeting.
6. Stop and review. The standalone app saves a disk copy. Attach original logs
   and save an updated disk copy, then share the reviewed evidence when online.

Record the GCS/application window as the screen input; share the recorder's
live view with the meeting. Selecting the recorder as its own recording input
can create a repeating screen image. Use the meeting microphone for team
conversation. Optional recorder narration is a separate local capture; camera
and microphone availability depends on the browser, OS and other active apps.

Meeting audio, participant chat and remote controls are not automatically
recorded or imported. Team members give feedback in the meeting; the operator
marks it in the session. This release provides a presentation view and uses
your existing meeting, without a hosted streaming relay or meeting bot.

No internet is needed to open the installed app, monitor local telemetry,
record, replay, prepare reports or export. A live remote meeting and uploads
need a connection. A lost aircraft telemetry link is separate from internet
availability. Observation age shows when incoming data stops; buffer gaps and
closed inputs are reported separately and do not establish radio packet loss.
