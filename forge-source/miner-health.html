How the pipeline works
Every day the pipeline fans out across the registered sources below — frontline conflict reporting, defense trade wires, federal procurement records, regulatory dockets, public-safety DFR programs, and component vendor catalogs. Raw articles, contract awards, and part listings are normalized into a common schema, deduplicated, and scored. That corpus is what feeds the flags, briefs, gray-zone scores, trend lines, and predictions published on this site — nothing here is hand-curated after the fact.
Data provenance & credit Community and public sources mined by the pipeline include: Militarnyi, Ukrinform, Defense Express, United24, Anadolu Agency and Daily Sabah (conflict reporting) · DefenseScoop, Breaking Defense, Defense News, The War Zone, War on the Rocks, Naval News (defense wire) · USAspending.gov, NATO NSPA, DIU, SBIR/STTR (procurement) · FCC, ITAR/EAR, Drone Watch EU (regulatory) · DRONELIFE, sUAS News, Unmanned Airspace, DJI Enterprise, Skydio, Auterion (commercial UAS) · Police1, FAA BEYOND, DHS AEL, DroneResponders, Grants.gov (public-safety DFR) · iFlight, Foxeer, Matek, RunCam, HGLRC, Holybro, T-Motor, Team BlackSheep, ARK Electronics, mRo and other vendors (parts & pricing).
