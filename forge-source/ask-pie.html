Cited retrieval · no generated conclusion

Ask PIE for the evidence—not a confident-sounding answer.

Enter a question about an actor, platform, company, program, component, TTP, procurement action, or supply-chain issue. PIE returns a deterministic evidence packet with source links, dates, match reasons, coverage, and limitations.

Retrieval contract
  • No LLM writes the answer or changes the ranking.
  • Every cited result links to the source URL stored in the corpus.
  • Direct means every meaningful query term matched—not that the claim is true.
  • Contradictions and source independence are not automatically resolved.
  • Missing results do not establish absence.

Do not treat retrieval as adjudication. This tool does not determine whether a report is true, whether two publishers are independent, whether an actor is responsible, whether a component is compliant, or whether an indexed gap represents an actual capability gap.
